·· Narrative

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the ACAD Lab at the New Jersey Institute of Technology, specializing in AI-driven electronic and photonic design automation. My research centers on a fundamental pipeline: Generate, Simulate, Verify, Correct. By leveraging generative machine learning, I aim to respect the underlying physics of circuits while generalizing across topologies—moving away from treating CAD as a simple black-box regression. From developing agentic workflows for TPU design to extending physics-informed diffusion models into RF phase shifter synthesis and silicon photonics, my work bridges the gap between modern AI capabilities and the rigorous constraints of hardware architecture.
