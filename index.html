·· about

I'm a PhD candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at NJIT, working in the ACAD Lab under Prof. Shaahin Angizi on AI-driven hardware design automation. My research treats circuit synthesis as a loop — generate → simulate → verify → correct — where LLMs and graph-based generative models propose candidate topologies and parameters, physics simulators check them, and failures feed back into the next generation. That framing unifies SPICEPilot, TPU-Gen, LIMCA, ARCANE, PICasso, and G-DiffPS, spanning SPICE-level analog synthesis, custom AI-accelerator generation, in-memory computing architecture exploration, and physics-informed graph diffusion for RF phase shifters. I currently split my time between the ACAD Lab and a research internship at Cognichip.
